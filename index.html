Why Early Detection Matters

⚠️

Late Detection

👨‍🌾

Limited Expert Access

Extension workers and diagnostic labs cannot immediately reach every affected farm.

🌦

Fragmented Information

🧪

Incorrect Treatment

Wrong diagnosis leads to delayed treatment, higher costs and yield destruction.
